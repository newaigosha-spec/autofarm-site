AutoFarm

A personal desktop tool for producing short vertical videos and uploading them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Runs locally on the owner's computer. There is no hosted service and no user registration.
  • Prepares a video file with its title, description and tags.
  • Uploads the video to a YouTube channel that the owner has connected through Google sign-in.

How it uses Google data

AutoFarm asks for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and to read the channel's name. It uses this access only to upload the videos you created in the app and to show which channel is connected. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
