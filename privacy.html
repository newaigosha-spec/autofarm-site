Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

AutoFarm is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer. It has no server of its own: nothing you connect to it is sent to the developer or to any third party other than the platforms you publish to.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel through Google sign-in, AutoFarm requests two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to upload the videos you prepared in the app to your channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — to read the channel's ID and name so the app can show which channel is connected.

AutoFarm does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, email or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • The channel name and ID are shown in the app's list of connected accounts.
  • The access is used only to upload videos that you chose to publish, with the title, description and tags you see in the app.

Storage

The authorization token issued by Google, the channel name and the channel ID are stored only in a local database file on the computer where AutoFarm runs. They are not copied anywhere else.

Sharing

AutoFarm does not sell, transfer or share your data with anyone. Data leaves your computer only as requests to the YouTube API that are needed to upload your videos.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Remove the account in the app's “Connections” page: the stored token is deleted from the local database.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AutoFarm's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. AutoFarm uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
